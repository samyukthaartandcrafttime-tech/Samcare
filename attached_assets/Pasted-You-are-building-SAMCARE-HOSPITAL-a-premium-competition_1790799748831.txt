# 9. RAPID ASSIST SAFETY AND MEDICAL LANGUAGE

Rapid Assist is a prototype support system.

It must distinguish:

## WHAT IS VISIBLE

from:

## WHAT REQUIRES CLINICAL CONFIRMATION

For image analysis, possible observations include:

* affected body area
* visible swelling
* visible bruising/discoloration
* visible wound
* redness
* apparent deformity
* other clearly visible observations

Example:

**Affected area:** Hand

**Visible observations:** Localized swelling, visible discoloration, superficial wound visible

**Preliminary concern:** Musculoskeletal injury may require clinical assessment

**Confidence:** Moderate

**Recommended next step:** Clinical evaluation recommended

Never claim:

“Fracture detected”

from an ordinary image alone.

Never claim:

“Infection detected”

from an ordinary image alone.

Never claim a definitive disease diagnosis from a simple uploaded file.
